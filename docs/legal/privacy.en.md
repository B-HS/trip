# Privacy Policy (draft)

Effective date: 2026-09-12

Trip processes the minimum information needed to provide the service, including email, username, password hash, itineraries, posts, and security logs. We use it for authentication, service delivery, security, and abuse prevention. We delete information when the account is closed or the purpose ends unless law requires retention. Users may request access, correction, or deletion. This draft must be reviewed before production publication.
